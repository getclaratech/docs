You've been choosing between software that's easy to start but impossible to change, and software that's flexible but takes months to set up. Clara is the third option: **a production-ready immigration CRM you can reshape as you grow.**

## Who is Clara for

- **Immigration law firms** — you need one system for matters, clients, documents, deadlines, and team collaboration.
- **Operations-heavy legal teams** — you want workflows and reminders that reduce manual follow-up without sacrificing control.
- **Privacy-conscious organizations** — you need to self-host and own your data end to end. Regulatory, contractual, or just principle.
- **Firms with technical operators** — you want a system you can adapt to your intake, case types, and internal processes as the firm evolves.
- **Implementation partners and internal product teams** — your team knows TypeScript, React, and PostgreSQL and wants to extend Clara without learning a proprietary stack.

## Who Clara is not for


- **Teams that want a CRM they never have to think
  about.** Clara rewards teams that want to stay close to
  their tools and shape them over time. If you want
  something fully managed, Pipedrive or
  HubSpot will serve you well.
- **Companies that need hundreds of pre-built integrations
  today.** Our ecosystem is growing fast, but it's not yet
  as broad as Salesforce or HubSpot. If you're comfortable
  building what's missing, you'll love it. If not, give us
  another year.
- **Organizations where tools are chosen in boardrooms,
  not by the people using them.** We don't do steak dinners
  and executive briefings. We win with teams that have the
  authority to pick their own tools.
